COURTYARD & PAVILION
Housed within a 19th century colonial bungalow and perched atop the Telok Blangah Hill,
The Alkaff Mansion is the biggest standalone multi-concept lifestyle destination in the South.
Your wedding reception sets the stage for an unforgettable celebration. Imagine welcoming your guests with a stunning
outdoor reception before exchanging vows at our iconic European-style pavilion. For a truly grand experience, consider
the breathtaking solemnisation at the majestic waterfall of The Grounds. Create cherished memories in
a setting that promises romance and elegance at every turn.

DINING
The second level is dedicated to the restaurant and arguably the most romantic place in the mansion with its ebony-hued timbers, contemporary lighting
fixtures that bathe the interiors in warm yellow light, and a view overlooking the lush greenery. The floor plan centers on a spacious dining hall, with 2 private
rooms easily created through sliding glass panels if privacy calls.
A rich patina of romance washes over the restaurant, from the lovingly restored ebony-hued timbers of the pitched ceiling and floors to the gorgeous,
