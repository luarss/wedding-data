MENU FAQ
General Questions Leftovers & Takeaways
Is tableware provided? Are takeaway boxes provided?
Yes, a full set of biodegradable, eco-friendly disposable ware is included. No, takeaway boxes are not provided due to Singapore Food Agency (SFA)
food safety regulations.
Is food tasting available? Can leftovers be consumed after the event?
No, food tasting is not available for buffet packages. For food safety reasons, we strongly advise against consuming leftovers after
the event.
Are there additional charges?
A 10% service charge and prevailing GST will apply to the total bill.
