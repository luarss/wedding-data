WEDDING KIT

Nestled at the heart of Sentosa Sensoryscape, 1-Flowerhill offers
a captivating haven for couples seeking a serene and intimate
solemnisation. Cocooned by lush greenery and tropical foliage,
the venue reflects the tranquil beauty of its surroundings,
creating a picturesque setting for your vows.

Step inside, and a timeless ambiance embraces you, where rich
heritage meets nature’s grace. Vibrant flowers and the soft
melodies of birdsong add a touch of enchantment, making every
moment feel poetic.

At 1-Flowerhill, your solemnisation becomes more than a
ceremony, it’s a celebration immersed in nature and history,
creating memories that linger long after the "I do.

SolemnisationRetreat into 1-Flowerhill’s inviting indoor spaces, where floralelegance and lush greenery set the stage for unforgettable moments. Choose between Wildseed Café on the first level, offering a cosy,floral-inspired setting perfect for relaxed gatherings, or Camille on the second level, where elegant décor and soft lighting create a refined ambiance for intimate dining. Floral arrangements adorn the tables and walls, infusing the space with the changingseasons' natural beauty. This versatile setting invites you to savour every moment with yourloved ones, creating the perfect backdrop for meaningful conversations and unforgettable memories. Indoor DiningThe wedding menu offers a refined fusion of French elegance andJapanese simplicity, creating a dining experience tailored toindividual tastes. Indulge in the bold, rich flavors of Frenchculinary traditions, complemented by the delicate and subtlenuances of Japanese cuisine. Each dish is thoughtfully crafted tosuit your vision, with the flexibility to design a custom menu thatperfectly reflects personal preferences.
