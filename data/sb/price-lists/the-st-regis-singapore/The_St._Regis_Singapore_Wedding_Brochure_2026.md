John Jacob
Ballroom

Venue
A grand, pillarless 709-square metre space with
soaring ceilings, two skylights and outfitted with
cutting-edge technology and LED walls for a dramatic
march-in and beautiful wedding video montage, the
John Jacob Ballroom is perfect for majestic banquets
of up to 380 guests.

An exclusive pre-event foyer furnished with a
built-in bar provides the perfect setting for elegant
cocktail receptions.

Exquisite Details
High ceilings adorned with two skylights flood the
ballroom with natural daylight, while hand-cut
crystal chandeliers and botanical motifs in the
hardware and furnishings create an atmosphere of
timeless sophistication. The ballroom is equipped with
two LED walls measuring 9.6-metres by 5.29-metres,
ten intelligent lighting systems and a state-of-the-art
sound system.

Caroline’s
Mansion

Venue
Named in honour of Mrs Caroline Astor, the
founding matriarch of the St. Regis brand, this venue
draws on the residential elements in her Upper East Side
home in New York, which played host to high society
events attended by Manhattan’s elite in the Gilded Age.

Located adjacent to the main hotel building, the
370-square metres Caroline’s Mansion offers a private
entryway, ceiling heights of 9-metres, a pre-event foyer
and a grand staircase - well suited for more intimate
wedding celebrations of up to 150 guests.

Exquisite Details
With intricately sculpted ceilings and accents like
floor-to-ceiling mirror paneled doors, pendant lights
with glass-blown crystals, arched brass fittings, timber
wood marquetry and custom-made carpets, Caroline’s
Mansion is the epitome of timeless elegance and
modern luxury.

Signature Services

Your wedding is a singular occasion reflecting the distinct taste of you
and your partner. Every detail of the ceremony can be customised to
your unique preferences, creating a personalised celebration and a
memorable experience for your guests.

The St. Regis Butler
Allow our dedicated butlers to take care of your every
need. First introduced at The St. Regis New York by
John Jacob Astor IV, the anticipatory and personalised
St. Regis Butler Service has remained a signature
offering of the brand for more than a century.
Complimentary butler service includes:
• Packing and unpacking of your luggage
• Beverage service upon arrival and in the mornings
   between 6:00 AM to 11:00 AM
• Daily garment pressing of two pieces daily; or up
   to four pieces per room
• Shoe Shine service
• Butler Service Desk and E-butler Services

Bridal Assistant
A dedicated bridal assistant is present with every couple
exclusively on their special day, lending anticipatory
service and tailored assistance each step of the way.

St. Regis Rituals
Our signature St. Regis Rituals are true to the spirit of
legendary events and unforgettable moments. From a
Champagne Sabrage to the Bloody Mary, tailor a
St. Regis ritual to your celebration for an elegant
addition to the occasion.

Champagne sabreing
A ritual which dates back to the battles of Napoleon
Bonaparte, who famously opened champagne with his
sabre in both victory and defeat.

The Bloody Mary
Perfected by famed barman Fernand Petiot in 1934 at
The St. Regis New York’s King Cole Bar, this signature
tipple remains the brand’s signature cocktail. Our local
twist, The Straits Mary is a smoky deconstructed riff
featuring bak kwa-infused whisky, chilli padi, and a
medley of spices.
