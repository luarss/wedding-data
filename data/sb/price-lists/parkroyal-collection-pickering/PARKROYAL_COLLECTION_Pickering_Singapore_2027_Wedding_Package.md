Every wedding brings family and friends together , sharing meals that become
cherished memories long after the day has passed. At PARKROYAL COLLECTION Pickering,
we believe the dining experience should be just as meaningful as the celebration itself.
With over two decades of culinary experience, award -winning Chef Chong Yee Foong brings
passion and finesse to our Chinese wedding menus. Inspired by authentic Chinese cuisine and
traditional Hakka dishes, he draws from heritage recipes, adding refined contemporary touches that
make every dish both familiar and special. Recognised internationally for his craft,
Chef Chong’s creations honour tradition while capturing the joy of gathering with loved ones.
