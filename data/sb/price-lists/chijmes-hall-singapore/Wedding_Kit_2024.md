ABOUT US

Watabe Wedding Group's origins trace back to a kimono rental
shop in 1953. Since then, it has evolved into a premier bridal
company, offering diverse wedding services globally in locations
such as Japan, Guam, Hawaii, Bali, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Europe,
and Singapore. With over 24 chapels and 12 hotels worldwide,
they've facilitated weddings for over 430,000 couples, averaging
30,000 couples annually.

Established on January 5th, 2011, Watabe Singapore Pte Ltd
operates as a subsidiary of Watabe Wedding Corporation,
overseeing operations at Chijmes Hall and Alcove at Caldwell
House. As the official venue operator Watabe Wedding Singapore
provides comprehensive wedding planning services, ensuring a
holistic Wedding Experience.

CHIJMES HALL
CHIJMES Hall represents the epitome of sophistication, providing an unparalleled venue for the realization of your dream wedding.
Erected in 1901, this 19th-century neo-gothic chapel has since been designated as a revered National Monument of Singapore, owing
to its striking architectural features. Within its pristine interior, crystal chandeliers adorn the lofty ceilings, while natural light filters
through intricate stained glass windows, bestowing an ambiance of majestic beauty. This immaculate setting offers couples the
perfect milieu to exchange vows and commence their matrimonial journey amidst an atmosphere of unparalleled grandeur.

ALCOVE AT CALDWELL HOUSE

Today, the upper level interior still features an original wall verse,
PARFAIT” in French, that
 translates to “Walk Along With Me And Be Perfect”. This brings a deeper meaning to have an intimate wedding held here.
