5-COURSE WEDDING FUSION SET DINNER MENU
STA RTER
Scallops Timbale
Perfectly seared scallops layered in a delicate timbale, paired with crispy tempura baby crabs.
Served alongside yuzu-marinated pink radish, fresh ice lettuce, and finished with a light ponzu
dressing for a harmonious blend of textures and flavors.
VEGETARIAN OPTION:
Avocado & Beetroot Timbale (V)
Layers of creamy avocado and roasted beetroot, served with crispy tempura zucchini blossoms.
Accompanied by yuzu-marinated pink radish, fresh ice lettuce, and finished with a tangy ponzu
dressing for a refreshing and elegant vegetarian starter.
SOU P
Creamy Butternut Squash Soup (V)
A velvety smooth blend of roasted butternut squash, delicately seasoned with herbs, and finished
with a touch of cream. Served with a hint of nutmeg and garnished with toasted pumpkin seeds
for added texture and flavor.
EN TRÉE
Pumpkin Gnocchi (V)
Handmade pumpkin gnocchi tossed in sage-infused brown butter, served with roasted walnuts and
a sprinkle of parmesan. Accompanied by charred broccolini for a savory, hearty twist on a classic
Italian dish.
MA IN S
Grilled Wagyu Beef Tenderloin
Succulent wagyu beef tenderloin grilled to perfection, served with truffle mashed potatoes, charred
broccolini, and roasted baby carrots. Finished with a rich red wine jus and a sprinkle of microgreens
for an elegant presentation.
VEGETARIAN OPTION:
Grilled Portobello Mushroom Steak
Served with a balsamic glaze, roasted root vegetables, and quinoa.
DESSERT
Coconut & Lime Panna Cotta (V)
A creamy panna cotta made with coconut milk and flavored with lime zest, set with agar-agar.
Served with a mango and passionfruit coulis on top, garnished with fresh mint leaves for a zesty
twist.
