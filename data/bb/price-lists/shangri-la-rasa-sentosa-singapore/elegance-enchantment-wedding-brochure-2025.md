Surrounded by spectacular views of the sea and resort landscape,
Horizon Pavilion is an ideal venue with floor-to-ceiling windows
that offers natural daylight, and magnificent day and night scenes.
 The venue seats 320 guests banquet-style, and is equipped with
intelligent lighting system, soundproof walls and audio visual equipment.

Siloso Ballroom

Siloso Ballroom is a pillarless function space, offering flexibility for any
wedding theme. Decorated in warm hues of maroon enhanced by the
subtle glow of the chandeliers, it is the largest event venue at the resort,
accomodating up to 360 guests banquet-style.
Siloso Ballroom is located on the ground level with easy access to the
resort's tropical landscaped gardens, car park and Siloso Beach.

Rasa Lawn

Embraced by the best of nature, Rasa Lawn offers a seaside setting for
an enchanting outdoor wedding with a mesmerising backdrop of the
South China Sea, gorgeous sunset view and tropical garden.
Celebrate your matrimony at this romantic venue that
seats up to 240 guests banquet-style.

Rasa Shore

Rasa Shore is situated right by the sea, accompanied by rustling
palm leaves and calming sea breeze for a perfect outdoor wedding.
Fill your joyous celebration with sandy beach vibes as you dine
al fresco with up to 340 guests banquet-style.
