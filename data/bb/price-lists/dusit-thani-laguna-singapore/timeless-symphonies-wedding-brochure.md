Your Love Story
At Dusit Thani Laguna Singapore

From an intimate celebration to a lavish affair, Dusit Thani Laguna Singapore
offers an exceptional setting where Thai-inspired graciousness meets
modern elegance. Our stunning venues and versatile function spaces —
framed by lush greenery and views of our two 18-hole Championship Golf
Courses, our beautifully designed venues and exquisite cuisines set the
stage for your dream wedding.

With over 4,000 sqm of versatile indoor and outdoor spaces, every detail
is thoughtfully curated to create a celebration that is uniquely yours. For
an unforgettable touch, selected days offer the rare opportunity for pre-
wedding and wedding shoots on our 18-hole Championship Golf Courses
amidst breathtaking scenery.

Pool Pavilions
Spanning 240 sqm, these Pool
Pavilions offer an intimate and
luxurious setting for your special
celebration. Each two-storey, three-
room pavilion is bathed in natural
light, featuring a private indoor
space with serene poolside views, a
refreshing plunge pool, and inviting
semi–alfresco lounge areas—ideal
for gatherings of up to 40 guests.

Blending privacy, flexibility, and
resort-style sophistication, the
Pool Pavilions transport you to a
tropical haven surrounded by lush
greenery, creating a perfect setting
for exclusive yet relaxed moments
shared with those dearest to you.

Laguna Ballroom
The Laguna Ballroom offers 8,073 sq. ft (750 sqm) of elegant and luxurious
space, ideal for hosting grand wedding celebrations. Its spacious, pillarless
design and soaring ceilings create a sense of openness and grandeur,
providing unobstructed views of the couple from every angle.
Beautifully appointed and highly versatile, the ballroom can be configured
into three individual spaces or enjoyed as one magnificent venue—perfect
for occasions of any scale, from intimate gatherings to lavish banquets.

Two 18-hole Golf Courses
Located on the same grounds as the acclaimed Laguna National Golf Resort
Club, a private members-only club, Dusit Thani Laguna Singapore offers

keepsake, our wedding team will be delighted to assist with arrangements for
exclusive pre-wedding photoshoots on the course available on selected days.
